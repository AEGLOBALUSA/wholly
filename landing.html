Built by the Church, for the Church

The people behind WHOLLY

WHOLLY was created by Pastor Ashley Evans and the team at Futures Church (Adelaide, South Australia) — a Pentecostal church with roots going back to 1922.

After years of watching incredible singles in our churches struggle with dating apps that ignored what mattered most — faith, character, and calling — we built the platform we wished existed. One where substance comes before selfies, and where your community can vouch for who you really are.

Our founding community includes members from Futures Church (Adelaide) and Planetshakers Church (Melbourne), with believers joining from across Australia, Singapore, and beyond.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 29, 2026

1. Who We Are

WHOLLY is a faith-first matchmaking platform operated by Futures Global Ltd, based in Adelaide, South Australia. Contact: ae@futures.global

2. Information We Collect

Account Information: Name, email address, age, gender, church affiliation, city, and profile photos.

Questionnaire Responses: Your answers to our 52-question compatibility assessment, used exclusively for matching purposes.

Usage Data: Interactions such as matches viewed, messages sent, and interests expressed.

Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and cookies for session management and analytics.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To generate compatibility matches using our five-dimension algorithm
  • To facilitate messaging between mutual matches
  • To process pastoral verification requests
  • To improve our platform and user experience
  • To send service-related notifications (you can opt out of marketing emails)

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL) with row-level security (RLS) encryption. Only you and your mutual matches can access your profile information. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. All data transmission uses TLS/SSL encryption.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

We may share limited data with:

  • Supabase (database hosting)
  • Netlify (website hosting)
  • Google Analytics (anonymized usage data)
  • Payment processors (for subscription billing only)

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data at any time via your dashboard
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

To exercise any of these rights, email ae@futures.global.

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management, and analytics cookies (Google Analytics) to understand how users interact with our platform. You can disable analytics cookies in your browser settings.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days. Anonymized analytics data may be retained indefinitely.

9. Children

WHOLLY is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated via email or an in-app notification. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries: ae@futures.global
